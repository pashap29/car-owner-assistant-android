# User Personas

## Persona A: Daily Driver (Primary)
### Context
Uses one car daily and wants predictable maintenance.

### Goals
- Avoid overdue service.
- Keep simple, reliable history.

### Pain Points
- Scattered records.
- Inconsistent odometer tracking.

## Persona B: Cost Optimizer
### Context
Tracks monthly cost and fuel efficiency closely.

### Goals
- Understand total cost per period.
- Detect unusual spikes quickly.

### Pain Points
- Incomplete logs.
- Low confidence in historical mileage.

## Persona C: Resale Planner
### Context
Needs credible ownership history before selling.

### Goals
- Show trustworthy timeline.
- Demonstrate service discipline.

### Pain Points
- Missing receipts.
- Conflicting odometer entries.

## Cross-Persona UX Needs
- Very fast data entry.
- Clear error recovery.
- Strong offline behavior.
- Localized units/currency/date formats.
